01 — Selected work

Problems first.
Pixels second.

Each project starts with who is stuck and why. Here’s the thinking behind four products I designed end to end.

2025–26 · SaaS dashboard

Uptime — hiring dashboards for trucking fleets

Product designDesign systemData-dense UI

Problem. Recruiters drowned in data and couldn’t see where candidates stalled.

Insight. Every role needs one question answered first: “what needs me today?”

Solution. Role-based dashboards with pipeline views, conversion analytics and AI suggestions on one design system.

Read case study →

2024 · Mobile app · Freelance

Chaze — wholesale ordering for retailers

UX & UIE-commerceAdmin portal

Problem. Shop owners ordered health products in person or by phone.

Insight. Many users weren’t comfortable with tech — every extra step lost an order.

Solution. A shortened ordering flow, clear hierarchy, custom icons and a matching admin portal.

Read case study →

2025 · Website · At Prixite

Prixite — a tech company site on one system

Visual designResponsiveVisual QA

Problem. Detailed wireframes, but no visual language to turn them into a polished, consistent site.

Insight. One shared system makes every new page faster to design and to build.

Solution. Full visual design in Figma, new pages on one system, tablet and mobile versions, and visual QA of the live build.

Read case study →

2025–26 · Marketing website

Uptime — one site for fleets and drivers

UI/UXWeb designTwo audiences

Problem. Fleets waste ad spend on unvetted applicants; drivers face slow replies and vague pay.

Insight. Each audience trusts different proof — fleets want metrics, drivers want pay and speed.

Solution. Separate value-first landing pages, real product previews and a 3-step driver onboarding.

Read case study →

03 — How I work

From brief to build, without the guesswork.

Four steps I repeat on every project — with AI speeding up research and exploration.

01

Empathize

Understand who I’m designing for, the business goal and the moment people get stuck — before drawing anything.

02

Ideate

User flows, information architecture and wireframes — several directions, compared early.

03

Design the system

High-fidelity UI in Figma on reusable components, so every page stays consistent as it grows.

04

Launch & test

Clean handoff, close work with developers, visual QA on the live build — then refine from feedback.
